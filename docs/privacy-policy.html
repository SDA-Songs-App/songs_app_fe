Privacy Policy

App: Hallelujah Songs (package com.sda.songs.hagarigna)
Developer: Hallelujah Songs
Last updated: October 8, 2026

Hallelujah Songs provides Seventh-day Adventist hymns and spiritual content in several Ethiopian languages. We respect your privacy. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your data.

1. Information we collect

The app does not require an account and does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or files. There are no advertisements and no analytics or tracking services in the app.

If you choose to send us feedback or suggestions through our Telegram group, the messages you send there are visible to us and are handled by Telegram under its own privacy policy.

2. Internet use

The app uses the internet only to download new songs and updates from our server and to check for app updates. When your device contacts our server or our update service (Expo), standard technical information such as your IP address, app version, and device platform may be received and recorded in server logs. We do not use this information to identify you.

3. Information stored on your device

Your selected language, font size and style, theme, favorite songs, and last-read song are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us or to anyone else.

4. Device permissions

The app requests only the permission to access the internet and to vibrate. It does not use your microphone, camera, location, contacts, or storage.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties. When you use the "share" feature, the song text you choose is passed to the app you select (for example Telegram); that sharing is under your control.

6. Children

The app is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

7. Accuracy of content

Song content comes from various sources and may contain errors. We welcome suggestions for corrections.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page and in the app.

9. Contact us

For questions or feedback: https://t.me/SDAStagingApp
